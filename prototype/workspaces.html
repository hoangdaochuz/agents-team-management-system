How workspace isolation works

Separate repos

Each workspace binds to one repo/folder. Agents can't read or write outside it.

Scoped agents & skills

Agents, skills, MCP servers, and rules belong to a workspace — no cross-workspace leakage.

Per-workspace members

Invite users per workspace and assign Owner / Admin / Member roles.
